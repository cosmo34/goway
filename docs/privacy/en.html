Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

GOWAY (“we”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the GOWAY mobile app for Montpellier’s TaM transit network.

1. Controller

Publisher of the GOWAY app. Contact: contact@picaza.fr.

2. Data we process

  • Location: used on-device to estimate walking times, plan trips, and update navigation / Live Activities.
  • Preferences: language, accessibility settings, favorite stop (widget), stored locally.
  • Technical identifiers: needed for notifications and iOS Live Activities.
  • Trip / search requests: sent to our API to compute itineraries (TaM GTFS / GTFS-RT open data).

3. Purposes

  • Show real-time schedules and routes.
  • Guide you during in-app navigation.
  • Send departure reminders (if allowed).
  • Keep the service reliable and secure.

4. Legal basis

Performance of the requested service and consent for location/notifications where required by the platform.

5. Retention

Location is not permanently stored for profiling. Preferences stay on your device until uninstall/reset. Server logs are kept only as needed for operations and security (limited period).

6. Sharing

We do not sell your data. No third-party ads. TaM / Montpellier Méditerranée Métropole open data is used under its license (ODbL). Apple services (notifications, Live Activities) may process technical data under their terms.

7. Transfers

Depending on API hosting, processing may occur in the EU. If a transfer outside the EU is required, appropriate safeguards will apply.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures (HTTPS in production, restricted access). No system is perfectly secure.

9. Your rights

Under GDPR: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability. Contact: contact@picaza.fr. You can also revoke Location / Notifications in iOS Settings.

10. Children

GOWAY is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page will change.
